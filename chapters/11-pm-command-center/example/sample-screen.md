# Sample Scenario A screen

At the top of the fictional Acme page, the PM sees `REVIEW_REQUIRED` and the knowledge as-of date. A notice says the risk assessment is older than the reporting run and that the dependency investigation has no dated run field.

The recorded-position cards show DEC-12/v2 PENDING from E-01, GH-41 as undated investigation context, and the reporting run's review state. The R1 item sits in a separate Hypotheses section, with its source reference visible on expansion. The human review queue asks the PM to confirm the formal decision state and refresh the risk signals. Component snapshots retain their individual statuses and dates.

There is no green release indicator, approval button, send button, or claim that R1 will slip. The page prepares a human decision; it does not make one.
