# Command Center view policy

All Acme records are fictional. This page is a local review aid. The Chapter 9 coordinator and Chapter 10 history remain the sources for its content; the page does not become a new source of project truth.

Show the knowledge as-of date, source references, and component dates or date gaps. Keep recorded decision state, undated investigation context, risk hypothesis, and proposed PM actions visually separate. If a required specialist is missing, show INCOMPLETE. If claims conflict, show CONFLICT. Do not turn either state into a routine status or forecast.

The renderer may read approved local JSON and write local `index.html` and `view-model.json`. It has no tool to approve decisions, close issues, send messages, update sources, or publish. Escape all dynamic source text before rendering HTML. The PM owns any action after review.
