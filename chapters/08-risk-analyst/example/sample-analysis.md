# Sample Scenario A interpretation

S-03 says Charlie's shared test environment is unavailable until November 16. Chapter 7's GH-41 investigation leaves DEC-12 unresolved. These are supplied scenario records, not observed results from a real project.

**Risk hypothesis:** If the contract approval and environment dates hold, Bravo's integration and Charlie's testing could lose their planned R1 window. The packet does not establish that R1 will be late or that Alpha's change caused Charlie's environment delay.

**Alternative explanation:** Bravo may complete independent work, and Charlie may secure another environment. Neither recovery path is confirmed in these records.

**Next action for PM review:** Confirm the v2 approval date and test-environment availability with their owners. Ask the teams for a fallback integration and test sequence. Record a formal risk and change the forecast only after the PM reviews the evidence.
